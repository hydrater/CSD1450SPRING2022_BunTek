Hey hey hey, welcome to our game.
It's a pong game with roguelike elemements.
You navigate through a randomly generated map and run into random encounters,
picking up artifacts that will upgrade your performance.
Beware, staying too long in a level will give you can be fatal.

You can find the survey link in the main menu or through this link here:

Notes and outstanding things:
1. Currently, you can press "I" to look at what artifacts you have
It will be accessible from buttons later on.
Enemies will also have a list of artifacts but they are unviewable
at the moment.

2. When you die, you load to the main menu
There are no telegraphs or feedback yet but it will be implemented soon

3. If you have resolution issues, 
you can full screen with F1

4. You can edit the game volume with arrow keys, it will be accessible
through a menu in beta with interactive sliders but for now:
Left and right arrow for editing music volume
Up and down arrow for editing sound fx volume
BE CAREFUL, THE VOLUME IS UNCAPPED

5. Aesthetics, visuals and audio
Other than the battle background placeholder, the art is all self made.
The music tracks are currently all placeholder as we will be making our own music.
We will be improving, see you guys in beta with a more polished version!
